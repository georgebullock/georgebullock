## **How I Work**

I do my best work in vague situations where the team needs clearer direction, deliberate trade-offs, and better decision logic. I like taking hard problems, breaking them down, and helping teams get to starting points everyone can commit to.

I care about good judgment more than process theater. Frameworks are useful when they help people make better decisions. They are a problem when they are substituted for critical thinking.

I bring strong measurement discipline from finance, marketing, and product. I care about instrumentation, analytics, and connecting product decisions to measurable outcomes. I also care about technical details. Caring about technical details does not mean I want to make engineering decisions. It means I want to understand technical trade-offs, collaborate better with engineers, and uncover technical risk earlier than I would have otherwise.

My communication style is direct. Over time, I’ve learned to adapt that directness to different people and situations. I still value clarity, honesty, and saying what I mean, but I’m more deliberate about my delivery than I was earlier in my career.

## **What I’m Looking For**

I’m focused on senior, staff, or lead product roles on the IC track. I’m especially interested in working on new products where the team is still working out what to build and there is enough complexity to make trade-offs interesting.

I’m not interested in being a people manager. I want to keep growing as a senior individual contributor, work with strong teams, and help build products that customers love.
